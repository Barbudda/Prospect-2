# Vision — Prospect Airbnb Leads V1

## One-liner
Automated B2B lead generation for solo founders selling to Airbnb-related operators.

## Problem
A solo founder selling an AI guest assistant needs to find
contactable Airbnb-related businesses fast. Current options are all broken:
- Manual Google searches = slow, tedious, impossible to scale
- Generic scrapers = fake data, not STR-focused, unreliable
- LinkedIn Sales Navigator = expensive, not STR-specific
- No tool exists that combines: search + extract + score + export for STR operators

## Solution
Enter a city. Click one button. Get a clean CSV of real,
contactable Airbnb-related leads with emails, phones, scores,
and outreach angles.

## V1 success criteria
- Find 30 to 100 real leads per city search
- At least 40% of leads have at least one real contact method (email or phone)
- Full run completes in under 10 minutes
- Zero fake or invented data anywhere
- One-click CSV export that works
- App builds without errors
- App runs without crashes
- App works with missing optional providers (graceful degradation)
- App works correctly with at least one configured search provider

## What this is NOT
- Not a CRM
- Not an AI demo
- Not a general scraper
- Not a marketing platform
- Not a manual workflow tool
